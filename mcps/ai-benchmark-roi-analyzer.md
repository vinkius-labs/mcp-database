## Description
This MCP server provides a strategic decision-support framework to quantify the economic value of AI industry benchmark achievements. It allows AI agents to calculate brand equity through `calculate_leadership_value`, estimate revenue gains via `calculate_conversion_impact`, determine financial efficiency with `calculate_roi_metrics`, and account for temporal relevance using `apply_shelf_life_decay`. It is designed to help organizations understand the return on their research investments in the competitive AI landscape.


## Available Tools (4)
- **calculate_conversion_impact**: Estimates the additional revenue or customer acquisition volume resulting from benchmark credibility
- **calculate_leadership_value**: Determines the total brand equity and market authority value generated by specific benchmark rankings
- **calculate_roi_metrics**: Provides the final financial efficiency metric by comparing total benefits to total costs
- **apply_shelf_life_decay**: Adjusts the calculated value of a benchmark achievement based on its age

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How does this tool calculate leadership value?**
The `calculate_leadership_value` tool determines brand equity by applying a prestige multiplier to the marketing value, which is then adjusted based on competitive pressure.

**Q: Can I account for the aging of benchmark results?**
Yes, you can use `apply_shelf_life_decay` to adjust the value of an achievement based on how many months have passed since it was recorded.

**Q: What metrics are provided for ROI analysis?**
The `calculate_roi_metrics` tool provides both the ROI percentage and the benefit-to-cost ratio.
