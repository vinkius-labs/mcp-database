## Description
This MCP server acts as an intelligent decision-support engine for automotive insurance claims. It reconciles repair estimates, approved-shop terms, parts preferences, vehicle availability, and policy conditions to generate a prioritized repair-selection checklist and a logical booking sequence. Use `compare_repair_options` to evaluate multiple estimates against policy limits, `validate_shop_eligibility` to verify authorized repair facilities, `check_vehicle_window` to ensure repair timelines fit availability, and `generate_booking_sequence` to create a step-by-step execution plan.


## Available Tools (4)
- **check_vehicle_window**: Determines if the proposed repair timeline fits within the vehicle availability
- **compare_repair_options**: Evaluates multiple repair estimates against policy constraints and user preferences
- **generate_booking_sequence**: Creates a step-by-step chronological plan to execute the chosen repair
- **validate_shop_eligibility**: Verifies if a specific repair shop is authorized under the current policy terms

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How does the tool evaluate repair options?**
The `compare_repair_options` tool evaluates estimates by checking if the total cost stays within policy coverage limits and how well they match user parts preferences.

**Q: Can I verify if a shop is in my network?**
Yes, you can use `validate_shop_eligibility` to check if a specific shop is authorized under your current policy terms.

**Q: What is a booking sequence?**
A booking sequence is a chronological plan generated by `generate_booking_sequence` that covers shop confirmation, part procurement, vehicle drop-off, and final inspection.
