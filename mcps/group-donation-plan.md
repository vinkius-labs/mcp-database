## Overview

**Category:** [finance](../categories/finance.md)

Calculates distribution of collective donation funds to causes based on pledges, matching rules, and targets.

## Description
This MCP server manages the complex logic of collective giving. It allows AI agents to calculate the net pool of funds after fees, apply matching rules to reward specific causes, and distribute the final amounts to recipients while respecting their target goals. Use `calculate_net_pool` to find available funds, `apply_matching_rules` to trigger bonus distributions, `distribute_funds` to finalize allocations, and `summarize_plan` to get a health overview of the donation drive.


## Available Tools (4)
- **calculate_net_pool**: Determines the total amount of money available for distribution after administrative fees are deducted
- **distribute_funds**: Performs the final calculation of how much money each cause receives
- **summarize_plan**: Provides a high-level overview of the entire donation plan's health and distribution
- **apply_matching_rules**: Calculates the extra funds generated by matching rules based on the distribution of pledges

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How are administrative fees handled?**
Fees are deducted from the total sum of pledges using the `calculate_net_pool` tool before any funds are distributed to causes.

**Q: Can a cause receive more than its target amount?**
The `distribute_funds` tool ensures that the allocated amount for any single cause does not exceed its defined target amount.

**Q: What are matching rules?**
Matching rules are logic sets that generate extra funds for specific causes when certain pledge thresholds are met, processed via `apply_matching_rules`.
