## Description
This MCP server provides a decision-support framework to translate technical AI rankings into measurable business value. By connecting AI agents to economic models, it calculates how benchmark positions influence customer acquisition, brand equity, and marketing efficiency. Use `get_benchmark_leadership_value` to determine prestige value, `calculate_customer_conversion_impact` to estimate funnel improvements, `evaluate_marketing_roi` for campaign efficiency, and `calculate_brand_equity_acceleration` to project brand growth.


## Available Tools (4)
- **calculate_brand_equity_acceleration**: Estimates the growth in the company's brand value resulting from the validation of their technical claims
- **calculate_customer_conversion_impact**: Estimates how much the benchmark success will increase the conversion rate of the existing marketing funnel
- **evaluate_marketing_roi**: Calculates the return on investment for marketing campaigns that leverage the benchmark results
- **get_benchmark_leadership_value**: Determines the total economic value generated solely by the prestige of the benchmark position

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How does this tool help with marketing strategy?**
It quantifies the ROI of marketing spend by calculating how technical validation from benchmarks increases customer conversion and brand value.

**Q: Can I calculate the impact of a Rank 1 position?**
Yes, using `get_benchmark_leadership_value`, you can determine the specific economic prestige generated by achieving the top rank.

**Q: What inputs are required for ROI calculation?**
To use `evaluate_marketing_roi`, you need the marketing investment amount, the leadership value from the prestige tool, and the projected customer acquisition impact.
