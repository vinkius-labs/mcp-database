## Description
This MCP server provides a suite of financial modeling tools to evaluate the economic viability of corporate innovation programs. Use `calculate_program_economics` to get a full economic snapshot, `evaluate_pilot_impact` to measure the value generated by successful pilots, and `analyze_funnel_efficiency` to assess the quality of startup attraction. It helps sponsors understand ROI, startup value, and program sustainability.

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How do I calculate the total ROI for my challenge?**
You can use the `calculate_program_economics` tool by providing the prize amount, application count, pilot conversion count, and your target ROI per pilot.

**Q: Can I evaluate the strategic value of my pilots?**
Yes, the `evaluate_pilot_impact` tool allows you to include a relationship value factor to account for strategic long-term partnership value.

**Q: How is funnel efficiency measured?**
The `analyze_funnel_efficiency` tool calculates conversion rates and cost per lead to determine recruitment efficiency.
