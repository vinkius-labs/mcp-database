## Description
This MCP server provides specialized tools for documenting and verifying the secure movement of sensitive family archives between custodians. It ensures a continuous and verifiable chain of custody through precise inventory management and integrity verification. Use `generate_transfer_list` to create master inventories, `create_coc_checklist` to generate step-by-step verification steps, and `verify_transfer_integrity` to confirm that no files were lost or altered during transit.


## Available Tools (4)
- **create_coc_checklist**: Generates a structured, step-by-step verification checklist
- **generate_transfer_list**: Creates a comprehensive inventory of all files designated for transfer
- **get_custodian_history**: Provides a chronological log of all past transfers involving specific custodians
- **verify_transfer_integrity**: Compares the intended transfer list against the actual receipt

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How do I ensure the records were not tampered with?**
You can use the `verify_transfer_integrity` tool to compare the intended transfer list against the actual receipt, checking both file counts and digital checksum matches.

**Q: Can I track the history of a specific custodian?**
Yes, the `get_custodian_history` tool provides a chronological log of all past transfers involving a specific custodian to audit their reliability.

**Q: What is a Chain of Custody (CoC) checklist?**
A CoC checklist is a structured set of verification steps generated by `create_coc_checklist` to track the movement of files and confirm they were handled correctly.
