Distribute supply costs to services and calculate profitability.

## Description
This MCP server connects AI agents to spa operational data to manage supply expenses. It allows for precise cost distribution using `get_allocated_costs` to assign product expenditures to specific services based on usage weights. You can also use `recommend_markups` to suggest optimal pricing for target margins, `calculate_service_profitability` to evaluate current service margins, and `summarize_supply_utilization` to get a high-level overview of how costs are distributed across your service menu.


## Available Tools (4)
- **recommend_markups**: Suggests pricing adjustments based on current service costs to reach target margins
- **summarize_supply_utilization**: Provides a high-level overview of how supply costs are distributed across the service menu
- **calculate_service_profitability**: Evaluates the actual profit generated by each service at its current price
- **get_allocated_costs**: Calculates how much supply cost is attributed to each service type

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How are supply costs distributed?**
Costs are distributed using the `get_allocated_costs` tool, which assigns portions of the total supply cost to each service based on its relative usage weight.

**Q: Can I get pricing suggestions?**
Yes, the `recommend_markups` tool suggests new prices to help you reach a specific target profit margin.

**Q: How do I check if a service is profitable?**
You can use `calculate_service_profitability` to compare the allocated service costs against the prices charged to customers.
