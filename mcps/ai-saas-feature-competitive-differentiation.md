## Description
This MCP server provides strategic modeling tools to quantify the competitive advantage of AI features. Use `analyze_feature_differentiation` to calculate differentiation scores and moat strength based on performance deltas and replication difficulty. Assess long-term viability with `assess_market_erosion` to account for open-source threats and innovation pace. You can also use `compare_competitor_landscapes` to map advantages against specific rivals or `simulate_moat_decay` to project how quickly a technical advantage might erode over time.


## Available Tools (4)
- **analyze_feature_differentiation**: Calculate the primary differentiation score and immediate market positioning for a specific set of AI features
- **assess_market_erosion**: Determine how external market factors like open-source availability will impact the feature's longevity
- **compare_competitor_landscapes**: Evaluate how a company's AI moat compares against multiple different competitors simultaneously
- **simulate_moat_decay**: Project the future state of the competitive moat over a specific time horizon

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How do I calculate my feature's differentiation score?**
You can use the `analyze_feature_differentiation` tool by providing your unique features, competitor parity, performance advantage percentage, and a replication difficulty rating.

**Q: Can I predict when my AI advantage will disappear?**
Yes, the `simulate_moat_decay` tool allows you to project the future state of your moat over a specific time horizon based on industry innovation rates.

**Q: How does open-source software affect my moat?**
You can use `assess_market_erosion` to determine how the availability of open-source alternatives impacts your feature's longevity and sustainability score.
